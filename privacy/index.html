Tally Mile Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Tally Mile records your drives so you can claim every mile you're owed. Your drives, places, and receipts stay on your phone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics trackers, and nothing is ever sold.

What Tally Mile uses, and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Location (GPS)Detects when a drive starts and ends, draws its route, and counts the miles without you doing anything.Stored on your phone. Sent to your own Apple Watch, if you have one, directly from phone to watch.
Map lookupsFinds a place you type or say, gets driving directions to your next stop, fills in street addresses for drives, and follows the road where GPS had no signal.The places involved are sent to Apple Maps, which answers under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive them.
Microphone and speechLets you log a drive or add a stop by talking, only after you tap the microphone.Speech is turned into text on your phone. The audio is not saved and is not sent anywhere.
Camera and photosLets you attach a photo of a gas or repair receipt.On your phone, only if you choose to add one.
Diagnostics logKeeps a short record of what drive detection saw (times, speeds, and app events, never locations) so a missed drive can be explained.On your phone. It leaves only if you tap Send a report, and then only to whoever you choose to email or share it with.
Purchase and subscription dataConfirms and manages a Tally Mile Pro subscription.Apple's App Store and our subscription service, RevenueCat, under their own privacy terms.
A random install IDStops a promo code from being redeemed twice on the same phone.Sent to our promo-code server (Supabase) when you redeem a code. It isn't tied to your name or anything else about you, and it changes if you reinstall.

What Tally Mile does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking services.
  • Your locations, drives, places, and receipts are never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data lives

Everything you record is kept in a database on your phone, the same way a notes app keeps your notes. The Home Screen widget and Lock Screen Live Activity read from it on the phone. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, that backup can include this data under Apple's iCloud terms. Deleting Tally Mile deletes this data from your phone.

Services we rely on

  • Apple: App Store purchases, Apple Maps lookups, and, if you use them, iCloud backups.
  • RevenueCat: checks and manages Tally Mile Pro subscriptions.
  • Supabase: our promo-code server. It never receives your locations, drives, or receipts.

Children

Tally Mile is made for drivers tracking mileage for work and taxes. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the way Tally Mile handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
